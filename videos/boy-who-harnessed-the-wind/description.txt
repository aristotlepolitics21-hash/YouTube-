The true story of William Kamkwamba, the boy who harnessed the wind. At 14, during Malawi's famine, he left school and built a working windmill from a bicycle, a tractor fan and scrapyard parts.

📖 The book: The Boy Who Harnessed the Wind by William Kamkwamba and Bryan Mealer.

His family couldn't pay his school fees, so William taught himself from a library book called Using Energy, mostly from the diagrams. His village called him "misala", crazy, for spending his days at the scrapyard. Then the blades turned and a bulb lit up in a house that had never had electricity. This video tells the story in under four minutes: how the windmill worked, exactly what he built it from, and why the hardest part wasn't the engineering.
