How Your Phone Battery Actually Works (5 Steps)

Every time you charge your phone, trillions of lithium ions make a journey, and back again. Here's what's going on inside, animated.

1. Two sides: graphite on one side, a lithium metal oxide on the other, a liquid electrolyte and a thin separator in between
2. Charging: the charger pushes lithium ions into the graphite, storing energy
3. Powering your phone: the ions flow back, and their electrons take the long way through your phone's circuits
4. Why it wears out: some ions get trapped in a layer that builds up on the graphite; many phones keep about 80% capacity after 500 to 1,000 full charges
5. Making it last: avoid heat, avoid sitting at 100% or running to 0%, and use your phone's 80% charging limit if it has one
