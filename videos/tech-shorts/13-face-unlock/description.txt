How Face Unlock Actually Works

When you glance at your phone, it can cover your face in thousands of invisible dots. Here's how it knows it's you, animated.

1. Invisible light: on phones with 3D face unlock, a flood light washes your face in infrared (so it works in the dark), then a projector beams 30,000+ infrared dots onto it
2. Reading the shape: an infrared camera photographs the dots; on a flat wall they'd sit in rows, but on your face they bend, and the phone builds a 3D map
3. Faces into numbers: the map becomes a mathematical description that's compared with the one saved at setup, all in well under a second. It isn't a photo, and it's kept in a secure chip on the phone
4. Real and paying attention: it can check your eyes are open and looking, and a flat photo won't fool real 3D sensing
5. Changing faces: it keeps updating for glasses, beards and hats. The chance a random stranger unlocks it is about one in a million (identical twins may manage it), and camera-only face unlock is easier to fool, so it's often not allowed for payments
