What Happens in the 1 Second After You Hit Enter

You type a web address and hit Enter, and the page appears in under a second. In that second your request can cross a city or an ocean and come back. Here is every step, animated.

1. Finding the address: browser cache, then DNS (resolver → root → .com → the site's name server)
2. The journey: packets, routers, and fibre-optic cables on the sea floor
3. The handshake: TCP (SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK), then TLS encryption, which is the padlock
4. The server answers: HTTP 200 OK, the HTML, then dozens more requests for CSS, JS and images
5. Drawing the page: DOM tree → styles → layout → paint
