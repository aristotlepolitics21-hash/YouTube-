13 Paintings, 81 Minutes, Still Missing | Animated True Crime

In 1990, two men dressed as police officers talked their way into Boston's Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum and stole 13 works of art worth around half a billion dollars. The empty frames still hang on the wall. This is the Gardner Museum heist, animated.

The knock: early on March 18, 1990, two "officers" said they were answering a disturbance call
The theft: both guards were handcuffed in the basement, and for 81 minutes the thieves cut paintings from their frames, including Rembrandt's only seascape and Vermeer's The Concert
The hunt: a $10 million reward, decades of leads and still no paintings
Still missing: the museum's founder wanted nothing changed, so the empty frames are still waiting
