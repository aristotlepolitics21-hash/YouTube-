The Grandpa Gang: The Hatton Garden Heist | Animated True Crime

Over Easter weekend in 2015, a gang of mostly elderly thieves (the oldest was 76) drilled through a concrete vault wall under London's jewellery quarter. This is the Hatton Garden heist, animated.

The vault: safe deposit boxes under Hatton Garden, and "Basil", the mystery man in a ginger wig who let the gang in
The drill: down the lift shaft, through half a metre of concrete, and stopped by a steel cabinet
The return: they came back two nights later, opened 73 boxes and took loot worth up to £14 million
The ending: the alarm went off but got no police response; the gang was rounded up about six weeks later, jailed in 2016, and Basil was convicted in 2019. Most of the loot has never been found.
