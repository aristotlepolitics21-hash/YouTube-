# Episode 6 as video: one clip per shot

For each shot, upload its image to an image-to-video tool as the **start frame**, paste the prompt, and pick **9:16** and **5 seconds** (10 for the longer lines). Save each clip with the shot's key (`hg_hook.mp4`, `hg_title.mp4` ...), or upload them in the chat in order and I'll name them.

Free or cheap tools that do this well: **Kling AI** (daily free credits), **Hailuo / MiniMax** (free credits), **Higgsfield** (free tier), **Pika**, **Runway**, **Vidu**. The tool's own sound isn't needed, because the narration, captions and cuts are added here.

Add this to the end of every prompt:
> Pixar-style 3D animation, smooth natural motion, keep the characters' faces and outfits exactly the same as the image, cinematic camera, no text, no subtitles
