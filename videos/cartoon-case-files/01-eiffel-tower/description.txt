The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower | Animated True Crime

In 1925, con man Victor Lustig "sold" the Eiffel Tower to a scrap metal dealer. It wasn't his to sell. Here's how the con worked, animated.

Paris, 1925: a newspaper story about the rusting tower gives Lustig an idea
The pitch: forged government letters and a secret meeting at the Hotel de Crillon
The bribe: why asking for a bribe made him look MORE real
The twist: the victim was too embarrassed to report it, so Lustig tried again
The ending: caught years later for counterfeiting, sent to Alcatraz, died a prisoner in 1947

Some details of this story (the number of dealers, the exact sums) differ between accounts; we've kept to the parts most sources agree on.
