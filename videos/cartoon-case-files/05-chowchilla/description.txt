The 26 Kids Who Dug Their Way Out | Animated True Crime

In 1976, a school bus full of children vanished on a quiet road in Chowchilla, California. This is the story of how bus driver Ed Ray and 14-year-old Michael Marshall dug everyone out, animated.

The road: July 15, 1976, with Ed Ray driving 26 children home from summer school when a van blocked the road
Underground: they were taken to a rock quarry and made to climb down into a buried trailer, held for ransom
The escape: Ed and Michael stacked mattresses, pried at the roof and dug, and every child climbed out alive
The ending: the three kidnappers were caught within weeks and given life sentences, and all three were paroled decades later

A bus driver and a 14-year-old who refused to give up.
