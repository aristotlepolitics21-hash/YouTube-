The Students Who Stole a King's Stone | Animated True Crime

On Christmas Day 1950, four Glasgow students broke into Westminster Abbey and took the Stone of Destiny, the stone that kings were crowned over. This is the story, animated.

The stone: once used to crown Scotland's kings, it was taken to London by Edward I in 1296 and built into the coronation chair
Christmas night: the students dragged it out of the chair, it broke in two, and they drove off with both pieces
The hunt: police set up roadblocks on the England–Scotland border, while a stonemason in Scotland secretly repaired the stone
The ending: in April 1951 it turned up at Arbroath Abbey draped in a Scottish flag, the students were never prosecuted, and the stone was officially returned to Scotland decades later
