The Sandwich That Cracked a Diamond Heist | Animated True Crime

In 2003, thieves emptied more than a hundred safe deposit boxes deep under the Antwerp Diamond Centre. Then a half-eaten salami sandwich gave them away. This is the Antwerp diamond heist, animated.

The vault: ten layers of security, including heat sensors, motion detectors, a magnetic field and a combination lock with a hundred million possibilities
The plan: Leonardo Notarbartolo rented an office in the building for over two years (and later claimed the crew rehearsed on a replica)
The night: Valentine's weekend, February 2003, and an estimated $100 million in diamonds, gold and jewels taken
The mistake: a bag of rubbish dumped in a forest beside the motorway, with vault tapes, receipts and a half-eaten sandwich inside
The ending: Notarbartolo was sentenced to 10 years, and most of the diamonds have never been found
