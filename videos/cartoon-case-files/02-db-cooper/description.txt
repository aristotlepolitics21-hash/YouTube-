The Hijacker Who Vanished: D.B. Cooper | Animated True Crime

In 1971, a man hijacked a plane, collected $200,000 in ransom, and jumped out into a stormy night. He was never seen again. This is the D.B. Cooper mystery, animated.

The flight: Thanksgiving eve, Portland to Seattle, and a note handed to a flight attendant
The swap: the ransom and four parachutes delivered in Seattle, and all 36 passengers released
The jump: the rear stairs lowered over southwest Washington in the dark and rain
The clue: in 1980, 8-year-old Brian Ingram found about $5,800 of the ransom money on a Columbia River beach
Still unsolved: the FBI ended its active investigation in 2016
