# Format analysis: @darkkesthistory (Instagram)

Taken from a profile screenshot on 17 October 2026: 84 posts, 11.7K followers, following 1, category "Community". Bio: "1000 Dark moments from history / Follow if you dare 💀". It also runs a broadcast channel ("HELLO 👋") with 1 member.

## The format

Every post is a Reel built on the same template:

- A black frame with the header **"1000 Dark Moments From History"**.
- A band of archival or colourised footage in the middle.
- An orange counter, **#N/1000**.
- One or two lines of bold white caption underneath.

## What works

- **The counter turns posts into a collection.** "#82 of 1000" says there is a long back catalogue and a lot more coming, which is a reason to follow rather than just watch.
- **The grid is instantly recognisable.** Every tile has the same black frame, header and orange number, so the profile reads as one series at a glance.
- **Anniversaries make it timely.** "87 years ago today" ties an old event to the day it's posted.
- **"Dark" is loose, and that's a strength.** A cat rescue and a man in a cabin sit next to Nuremberg. The real promise is moments that hit hard emotionally, not only tragedies.
- **It's cheap to make.** It's existing footage plus a caption, so it can post often.

## Weaknesses

- **Accuracy slips.** "1965-1962" is a typo. Dick Proenneke was born in May 1916, so he was 52, not 51, when he built the cabin in 1968. Two different posts are both numbered #82. Small errors like these invite "well, actually" comments and cost trust.
- **The footage isn't theirs.** On YouTube, channels built from reused clips with minimal changes risk being refused monetisation under the reused-content policy, and individual clips can be claimed.
- **Tiny text.** The captions are hard to read on the grid tiles.
- **It's one of many.** Plenty of accounts post colourised clips with captions, and nothing in the footage is unique to this one.

## How Dark Chapters uses this

The same mix of topics and the same numbered-series hook, but every frame is original 3D animation made with our own engine, and every script is checked against sources before it's voiced:

- Each video is our own work, so it's safe to monetise and distinct from repost accounts.
- The toy-diorama look handles dark subjects without showing graphic footage, which makes them safer for platforms and advertisers.
- **Dark Chapters #N** keeps the collection hook.
- Plan anniversary posts ahead of time, such as 1 September for WWII and 20 November for Nuremberg.

Topics I left out for now:

- **#81 (the women's bridge).** The post names no specific bridge and its dates contradict each other. Each needs a source before it gets a script.
