On 21 October 1966, a colliery waste tip slid down the mountain above Aberfan, in South Wales, and buried Pantglas Junior School. 144 people died, 116 of them children. The tribunal found it was not an act of God: the tip was built over springs, and the warnings had been ignored. This is the story of Aberfan.

Key facts in this film:
- The tip slid at about 9:15 AM on 21 October 1966
- 144 people died, 116 of them children
- Tip 7 was built over natural springs shown on old maps
- Local officials had written to the National Coal Board about the danger in 1963
- The Aberfan Tribunal blamed the National Coal Board; a new law on tip safety followed in 1969
