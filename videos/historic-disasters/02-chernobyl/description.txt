On 26 April 1986, reactor 4 at the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant exploded during a safety test. The emergency shutdown button, meant to stop the reaction, briefly made it worse. This is how the worst nuclear accident in history happened.

Key facts in this film:
- The explosion happened at 1:23 am on 26 April 1986, during a test of the cooling system
- The RBMK design was unstable at low power, and its control rods had graphite tips that briefly increased reactivity when inserted
- Pripyat (about 49,000 people) was evacuated about 36 hours later
- 31 deaths in the first months; 134 people diagnosed with acute radiation syndrome
- More than 300,000 people were eventually resettled from contaminated land
- The New Safe Confinement arch was placed over the reactor in 2016
