On 14 April 1912, Titanic struck an iceberg and sank in 2 hours 40 minutes. More than 1,500 people died. This documentary tells how it really happened: not as legend, but as a chain of decisions that each made sense at the time.

Key facts in this film:
- About 2,224 people aboard; 20 lifeboats with room for 1,178
- British rules required 16 lifeboats for any ship over 10,000 tons
- At least 7 ice warnings received on 14 April
- Struck the iceberg at 11:40 pm; sank at 2:20 am on 15 April
- The first lifeboat (Boat 7) left with 28 people in a boat for 65
- The Carpathia rescued 705 survivors
- The first International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea was signed in 1914
- The wreck was found in 1985, 3,800 metres down
