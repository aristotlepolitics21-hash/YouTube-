Just after midnight on 3 December 1984, a cloud of methyl isocyanate gas escaped from a pesticide plant in Bhopal, India, and settled over the sleeping city. Almost every safety system that could have stopped it was switched off, broken or too small. This is the story of the worst industrial disaster in history.

Key facts in this film:
- The leak began late on 2 December and peaked just after midnight on 3 December 1984
- Around 40 tonnes of methyl isocyanate escaped from tank E610
- The official death toll is 3,787; other estimates are far higher
- More than 500,000 people were exposed to the gas
- Union Carbide settled with the Indian government for $470 million in 1989
