On 20 April 2010, a blowout on the Deepwater Horizon rig in the Gulf of Mexico killed eleven workers and unleashed the largest marine oil spill in American history. Failed cement, a misread test and a blowout preventer that could not seal the well let oil flow for 87 days. This is the final episode of Historic Disasters.

Key facts in this film:
- The blowout happened at about 9:45 PM on 20 April 2010
- 11 workers were killed and 17 injured; 115 people were rescued
- Oil flowed from the Macondo well for 87 days until it was capped on 15 July 2010
- Estimates of oil released range from about 3.19 million to 4.9 million barrels
- BP agreed a $20.8 billion settlement in 2015
