On 6 May 1937, the German airship Hindenburg burst into flames while landing at Lakehurst, New Jersey. In about half a minute, the largest aircraft ever built was a burning wreck, filmed and recorded for the world to see. This is the story of the Hindenburg, and the end of the age of the airship.

Key facts in this film:
- The Hindenburg caught fire at about 7:25 PM on 6 May 1937 at Lakehurst, New Jersey
- 36 people died; 62 of the 97 people aboard survived
- It was filled with about 7 million cubic feet of hydrogen because helium was unavailable
- Investigators blamed leaking hydrogen ignited, most likely, by static electricity
- Herbert Morrison recorded the famous Oh, the humanity broadcast
