## Historical background

- ✅ Stamford Raffles arrived on 29 January 1819 and signed a treaty with Sultan Hussein and the Temenggong on 6 February 1819. [1](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/infopedia/articles/SIP_131_2005-01-03.html) [2](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/infopedia/articles/SIP_715_2004-12-15.html)
- ⚠️ uncertain About a thousand people lived on the island before Raffles arrived (estimates vary). [1](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/infopedia/articles/SIP_715_2004-12-15.html) — _estimates range from a few hundred to about 1,000_
- ✅ Singapore became part of the Straits Settlements in 1826 and their capital in 1836. [1](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/infopedia/articles/SIP_715_2004-12-15.html)

## Geography

- ✅ Land area was about 581.5 km² in 1960 and about 744 km² by the 2020s, mostly through reclamation. [1](https://data.gov.sg/dataset/total-land-area-of-singapore) [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Singapore)
- ✅ Singapore lies at the southern tip of the Malay Peninsula on the sea route between the Indian Ocean and the South China Sea. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Singapore)

## Political development

- ✅ Full internal self-government came with the May 1959 election; the PAP won 43 of 51 seats and Lee Kuan Yew became prime minister. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1959_Singaporean_general_election)
- ✅ Singapore joined Malaysia on 16 September 1963 and separated on 9 August 1965. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_State_of_Singapore_(Malaysia))
- ✅ Lee Kuan Yew called separation 'a moment of anguish' at a televised press conference on 9 August 1965. [1](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-22-issue-2-jul-sep-2026-the-days-leading-to-separation-in-lee-kuan-yews-own-words-/)

## Major historical events

- ✅ Singapore fell to Japan on 15 February 1942; about 80,000 Allied troops became prisoners. [1](https://www.sg101.gov.sg/learn/resources/onthisday-fall-of-singapore) [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fall_of_Singapore)
- ✅ Renamed Syonan-to; British rule returned in September 1945. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_occupation_of_Singapore)
- ✅ Britain announced in January 1968 it would withdraw forces east of Suez by 1971; most troops left by October 1971. [1](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1922414) [2](https://countrystudies.us/singapore/28.htm)
- ✅ The Bukit Ho Swee fire of 25 May 1961 left about 16,000 people homeless. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Development_Board)
- ✅ There were deadly communal riots in Singapore in 1964. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_State_of_Singapore_(Malaysia))

## Population

- ✅ Total population was 6.04 million in June 2024; about one in five citizens was 65 or older, rising to one in four by 2030. [1](https://www.population.gov.sg/files/media-centre/publications/Population_in_Brief_2024.pdf)
- ✅ Resident total fertility rate fell to 0.97 in 2023, below 1 for the first time. [1](https://asianews.network/?p=136624)
- ✅ Around 1968 the population was about 2 million. [1](https://countrystudies.us/singapore/28.htm)

## Natural resources

- ✅ Singapore has no significant oil, gas or mineral resources and little farmland. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography_of_Singapore)
- ✅ Water agreements with Johor were signed in 1961 and 1962; the 1961 agreement expired in 2011, the 1962 one runs to 2061. [1](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/infopedia/articles/SIP_1533_2009-06-23.html) [2](https://lkyspp.nus.edu.sg/gia/article/water-policy-in-singapore)
- ⚠️ uncertain NEWater and desalination meet a large and growing share of demand. [1](https://lkyspp.nus.edu.sg/gia/article/water-policy-in-singapore) — _published shares vary by year (around 40% in older reports); not quoted as a number_

## Economy

- ✅ GDP per capita was about US$517 in 1965 and about US$94,900 in 2024 (current US$, World Bank). [1](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/data/PCAGDPSGA646NWDB)
- ✅ Manufacturing is about a fifth of the economy. [1](https://www.statista.com/topics/9319/manufacturing-sector-in-singapore)
- ✅ About one in ten chips worldwide is made in Singapore (A*STAR). [1](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/News/astarNews/news/features/singapore-semiconductor-rise-ai-chip-wars)
- ⚠️ uncertain In 1959 roughly 14% unemployment was estimated. [1](https://www.sg101.gov.sg/economy/surviving-our-independence/1959-1965/) — _figure quoted inconsistently; not used in narration_

## Trade

- ✅ Trade was about 326% of GDP in 2023. [1](https://wits.worldbank.org/CountryProfile/en/Country/SGP/Year/LTST)
- ✅ The port handled a record 41.12 million TEU in 2024; around 90% is transshipment. [1](https://www.mpa.gov.sg/media-centre/details/strong-growth-momentum-for-maritime-singapore)

## Major industries

- ✅ Electronics, semiconductors, chemicals, precision engineering and finance are major industries. [1](https://www.a-star.edu.sg/News/astarNews/news/features/singapore-semiconductor-rise-ai-chip-wars) [2](https://www.statista.com/topics/9319/manufacturing-sector-in-singapore)
- ✅ Singapore ranks in the global top five financial centres on the Global Financial Centres Index. [1](https://www.finews.asia/finance/37717-singapore-surpasses-hong-kong-in-financial-hub-index)

## Infrastructure

- ✅ Changi Airport began operating on 1 July 1981. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changi_Airport)
- ✅ Tuas Port is designed to handle 65 million TEU when completed in the 2040s. [1](https://porteconomicsmanagement.org/pemp/contents/part3/changing-geography-of-seaports/singapore-tuas-port-expansion-project)
- ✅ Jurong Industrial Estate's first factory opened in August 1963. [1](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/jurong-industrial-estate/story)

## Government policies

- ✅ The Economic Development Board was set up on 1 August 1961, after a UN team led by Albert Winsemius advised on industrialisation. [1](https://www.sg101.gov.sg/economy/surviving-our-independence/1959-1965/) [2](https://www.roots.gov.sg/stories-landing/stories/jurong-industrial-estate/story)
- ✅ The HDB was created in 1960 and built about 21,000 flats in under three years. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Development_Board)
- ✅ From 1968, CPF savings could be used to buy HDB flats. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Housing_and_Development_Board)
- ✅ About 77% of residents live in HDB flats; home ownership is about 90% (2023). [1](https://www.singstat.gov.sg/modules/infographics/-/media/Files/visualising_data/infographics/Population/singapore-population10052024.pdf)
- ✅ The CPIB was set up in 1952. [1](https://www.cpib.gov.sg/who-we-are/our-heritage/)
- ✅ Temasek was incorporated in 1974; GIC was set up in 1981. [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temasek_Holdings) [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GIC_(sovereign_wealth_fund))

## International relations

- ✅ The British bases generated about 20% of GDP, employed 54,000 people directly and occupied 12% of the land. [1](https://countrystudies.us/singapore/28.htm) [2](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/node/1922414)

## Problems and challenges

- ✅ Low fertility and rapid ageing. [1](https://asianews.network/?p=136624) [2](https://www.population.gov.sg/files/media-centre/publications/Population_in_Brief_2024.pdf)
- ✅ Sea levels could rise by up to 1 m by 2100; coastal protection could cost S$100 billion or more (PM Lee, 2019). [1](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/world/2019/08/19/singapore-to-bolster-coastal-defences-against-rising-sea-levels-says-pm/1781949)

## Important statistics

- ✅ Singapore ranked 3rd of 180 in the 2024 Corruption Perceptions Index (score 84). [1](https://www.cpib.gov.sg/press-room/2024-ti-cpi-singapore-moves-up-2-spots-to-3rd-least-corrupt-country-and-top-in-asia-pacific/)
