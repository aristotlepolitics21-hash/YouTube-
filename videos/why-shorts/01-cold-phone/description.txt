Ever had your phone die in the cold with plenty of battery left? Your battery isn't empty. It makes power by moving lithium ions through a liquid, and in the cold that liquid gets thick like syrup. The ions slow down, the voltage drops, and your phone shuts itself off to stay safe. Warm it back up and most of that charge comes back.

Tip: in winter, keep your phone in an inside pocket, close to your body.
