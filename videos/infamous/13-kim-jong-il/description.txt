Key facts in this film:
- Born 16 February 1941 in the Soviet Far East, according to Soviet records; North Korea claims 1942 on Mount Paektu
- Publicly confirmed as Kim Il-sung's successor at the Sixth Party Congress in 1980
- South Korean actress Choi Eun-hee and director Shin Sang-ok were abducted in 1978 and escaped in Vienna in 1986
- Korean Air Flight 858 was bombed on 29 November 1987, killing all 115 people on board
- Took power after Kim Il-sung died on 8 July 1994
- Famine of 1994–1998: estimates range from about 240,000 to over 2 million deaths; many scholars estimate 600,000–1 million
- Admitted to Japan on 17 September 2002 that North Korea had abducted 13 Japanese citizens
- North Korea's first nuclear test took place on 9 October 2006
- Died 17 December 2011, according to state media

Sources: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Report of the UN Commission of Inquiry on Human Rights in the DPRK (2014), Stephan Haggard and Marcus Noland's Famine in North Korea, Bradley K. Martin's Under the Loving Care of the Fatherly Leader, BBC News.
