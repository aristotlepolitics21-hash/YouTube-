Henry VIII married six times, executed two of his wives, broke England away from the Church of Rome and closed more than eight hundred monasteries, all in the search for a male heir. This documentary follows the golden young prince who became one of England's most feared kings, and weighs what he left behind.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 28 June 1491 at Greenwich, the second son of Henry VII
- Became king in April 1509, aged 17, and married Catherine of Aragon that June
- Given the title 'Defender of the Faith' by Pope Leo X in 1521
- Married Anne Boleyn in January 1533; Elizabeth was born that September
- The Act of Supremacy (1534) made the king Supreme Head of the Church of England
- Sir Thomas More was executed on 6 July 1535; Anne Boleyn on 19 May 1536
- More than 800 religious houses were dissolved between 1536 and 1540
- Died at Whitehall on 28 January 1547, aged 55
