Grigori Rasputin has been called a mad monk, a hypnotist, the empress's lover and the man who destroyed Russia. Most of that is myth. This documentary separates the legend from the record: the Siberian peasant, the sick heir, the scandal and the murder in a palace cellar.

Key facts in this film:
- Born in 1869 in Pokrovskoye, western Siberia, to a peasant family
- Never a monk or a priest; he was a wandering lay pilgrim (strannik)
- First met Nicholas II and Alexandra on 1 November 1905 (Old Style)
- Heir Alexei, born 1904, had haemophilia; the illness was kept secret
- Stabbed and badly wounded in Pokrovskoye in the summer of 1914
- Killed at the Yusupov Palace, Petrograd, on 17 December 1916 (Old Style; 30 December New Style)
- The autopsy found he had been shot three times, including once in the forehead
- Nicholas II abdicated in March 1917; the family was killed in Yekaterinburg in July 1918
