Adolf Hitler was handed power legally in January 1933. Twelve years later, his regime had murdered six million Jews and Europe lay in ruins. This documentary tells how a failed art student from Austria rose to dictator, step by step, and how many people helped him on the way.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 20 April 1889 in Braunau am Inn, Austria-Hungary
- Rejected twice by the Vienna Academy of Fine Arts (1907 and 1908)
- Beer Hall Putsch, 8–9 November 1923; served about nine months of a five-year sentence
- Nazi vote rose from 2.6% (1928) to 37.3% (July 1932); never a majority in a free election
- Appointed chancellor 30 January 1933; Enabling Act passed 23 March 1933
- Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939
- About six million Jews were murdered in the Holocaust
- Died by suicide in the Führerbunker, Berlin, 30 April 1945; Germany surrendered 8 May 1945
