Heinrich Himmler looked like a mild, bespectacled clerk. As head of the SS, the police and the concentration camps, he did more than anyone except Hitler to organise the Holocaust. This documentary tells how an agriculture graduate built the machinery of Nazi terror, and how he tried to escape justice.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 7 October 1900 in Munich
- Took part in the Beer Hall Putsch in November 1923
- Appointed Reichsführer-SS on 6 January 1929, when the SS had fewer than 300 members
- Announced the opening of Dachau concentration camp in March 1933
- Became Chief of German Police on 17 June 1936
- Spoke openly of the 'extermination of the Jewish people' in a speech to SS officers at Posen on 4 October 1943
- Stripped of all offices by Hitler in April 1945 after secretly offering surrender to the Western Allies
- Died by suicide with cyanide in British custody near Lüneburg on 23 May 1945
