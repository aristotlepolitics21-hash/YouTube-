In May 1991, Milwaukee police officers handed a dazed fourteen-year-old boy back to Jeffrey Dahmer. This documentary is about the seventeen boys and young men he killed, and the many moments when someone could have stopped him. Nothing graphic is shown or described.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 21 May 1960 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin; grew up in Bath Township, Ohio
- First killing in June 1978: Steven Hicks, 18, a hitchhiker
- Convicted in 1989 of sexually assaulting a 13-year-old boy; given one year in a work-release jail and five years' probation
- Moved into the Oxford Apartments, Milwaukee, in May 1990; twelve of his victims were killed there
- 27 May 1991: police returned 14-year-old Konerak Sinthasomphone to Dahmer, who killed him shortly afterwards
- Arrested 22 July 1991 after Tracy Edwards escaped and flagged down police
- Found sane by a jury on 15 February 1992; sentenced to fifteen consecutive life terms, plus a sixteenth in Ohio
- Killed by a fellow inmate at Columbia Correctional Institution, Wisconsin, on 28 November 1994
