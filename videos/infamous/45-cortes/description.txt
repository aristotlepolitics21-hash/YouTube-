In 1519 Hernán Cortés landed in Mexico with about five hundred soldiers. Two years later, Tenochtitlan, one of the largest cities in the world, lay in ruins. This documentary tells how it happened, and why the old story of a few hundred conquerors leaves most of it out.

Key facts in this film:
- Born in 1485 in Medellín, Extremadura, Spain
- Sailed from Cuba in February 1519 with 11 ships, about 500 soldiers and 16 horses
- Entered Tenochtitlan and met Moctezuma II on 8 November 1519
- The Spaniards fled the city on La Noche Triste, the night of 30 June to 1 July 1520
- A smallpox epidemic in 1520 killed Moctezuma's successor, Cuitláhuac
- Tenochtitlan fell and Cuauhtémoc was captured on 13 August 1521
- Made Marquis of the Valley of Oaxaca in 1529
- Died at Castilleja de la Cuesta, near Seville, on 2 December 1547
