Saddam Hussein ruled Iraq from 1979 to 2003. He took his country into two disastrous wars, gassed Kurdish civilians at Halabja, and crushed every opponent he could find. This documentary tells how a boy from a poor village near Tikrit built one of the most feared dictatorships of his time, and how it fell.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 28 April 1937 in al-Awja, near Tikrit, Iraq
- Took part in a failed attempt to assassinate Iraq's ruler, Abd al-Karim Qasim, in October 1959
- Became president of Iraq in July 1979 and purged the ruling party days later
- Invaded Iran on 22 September 1980; the war lasted until August 1988
- Around 5,000 people were killed in the chemical attack on Halabja on 16 March 1988
- Invaded Kuwait on 2 August 1990; driven out by a US-led coalition in February 1991
- Captured near Tikrit on 13 December 2003
- Convicted of crimes against humanity over the Dujail killings on 5 November 2006 and executed on 30 December 2006
