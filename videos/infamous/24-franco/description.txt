Francisco Franco led the army revolt that plunged Spain into civil war in 1936, won it with help from Hitler and Mussolini, and ruled as dictator until his death in 1975. Tens of thousands of his opponents were executed, and Spain is still searching for the missing. This documentary tells his story and why Spaniards remain divided over it.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 4 December 1892 in Ferrol, Galicia
- Became one of Europe's youngest generals in 1926, aged 33
- The military uprising against the Spanish Republic began on 17–18 July 1936
- German and Italian aircraft bombed Guernica on 26 April 1937
- Franco declared the Civil War over on 1 April 1939
- Historians estimate around 500,000 deaths in the war, and well over 100,000 killings behind the lines and in the post-war repression by Franco's side
- Died in Madrid on 20 November 1975, aged 82
- His remains were moved from the Valley of the Fallen on 24 October 2019
