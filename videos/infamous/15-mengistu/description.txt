Mengistu Haile Mariam helped overthrow Ethiopia's last emperor in 1974 and ruled the country for seventeen years. His Red Terror killed tens of thousands or more, and his government's war tactics deepened one of the worst famines of the century. This documentary tells his story, and why, as of 2025, he has never served his sentence.

Key facts in this film:
- Emperor Haile Selassie was deposed by the military committee known as the Derg on 12 September 1974
- Sixty former officials were executed without trial on 23 November 1974
- Mengistu became sole leader of the Derg in February 1977
- The Red Terror of 1977–1978 killed tens of thousands; Amnesty International has estimated up to 500,000
- Somalia's invasion of the Ogaden (1977–1978) was defeated with Soviet arms and Cuban troops
- The famine of 1983–1985 killed an estimated 400,000 to 1 million people
- Fled to Zimbabwe on 21 May 1991
- Convicted of genocide in absentia by Ethiopia's Federal High Court in December 2006; sentenced to death on appeal in 2008
