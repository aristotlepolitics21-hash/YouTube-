Kim Jong-un became North Korea's leader in 2011, in his late twenties. Since then he has tested nuclear weapons, executed his own uncle, met an American president and sent troops to fight for Russia. This documentary sets out what is documented about his rule, including the findings of the 2014 UN Commission of Inquiry.

Key facts in this film:
- North Korea was founded in 1948; Kim Il-sung was its first leader, followed by Kim Jong-il (1994–2011)
- Kim Jong-un's birth date is given as 8 January; the year (1982, 1983 or 1984) is disputed
- Became leader after Kim Jong-il's death on 17 December 2011
- State media announced the execution of his uncle Jang Song-thaek in December 2013
- The 2014 UN Commission of Inquiry found crimes against humanity and estimated 80,000–120,000 political prisoners
- North Korea carried out four of its six nuclear tests under Kim Jong-un (2013, 2016 twice, 2017)
- Kim Jong-nam was killed with VX nerve agent at Kuala Lumpur airport on 13 February 2017
- Met Donald Trump in Singapore on 12 June 2018, the first meeting of a sitting US president and a North Korean leader
- North Korea confirmed in April 2025 that it had sent troops to fight for Russia
