In July 2014, a man almost nobody had seen stood in a Mosul mosque and declared himself caliph. Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi turned a beaten insurgent group into a self-declared state that enslaved Yazidi women, filled mass graves and inspired attacks around the world. This documentary tells how he rose, how his state fell, and what it left behind.

Key facts in this film:
- Born Ibrahim Awwad Ibrahim al-Badri in 1971 near Samarra, Iraq
- Detained by US forces at Camp Bucca in 2004; released in December 2004
- Became leader of the Islamic State of Iraq in May 2010
- Announced the Islamic State of Iraq and al-Sham (ISIS) in April 2013
- Group declared a 'caliphate' on 29 June 2014; he preached at Mosul's al-Nuri mosque on 4 July 2014
- Attack on Sinjar began 3 August 2014; a UN commission found genocide against the Yazidis in June 2016
- Mosul retaken July 2017; last territory lost at Baghouz, March 2019
- Died during a US special forces raid in Barisha, Syria, 26–27 October 2019
