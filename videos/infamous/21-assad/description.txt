Bashar al-Assad trained as an eye doctor in London and was never meant to rule Syria. When his people rose up in 2011, he answered with force, and the war that followed killed hundreds of thousands of people. This documentary tells how he came to power, how the war was fought, and how his regime collapsed in eleven days in December 2024.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 11 September 1965 in Damascus, son of Hafez al-Assad, president of Syria from 1971 to 2000
- Trained in ophthalmology in London before his elder brother Bassel died in a car crash in January 1994
- Became president in July 2000 after parliament lowered the minimum age from 40 to 34
- Protests began in March 2011; the UN human rights office estimated over 306,000 civilians were killed between March 2011 and March 2021
- A UN mission confirmed sarin was used in Ghouta on 21 August 2013
- A joint UN–OPCW mechanism found the Syrian government responsible for the sarin attack on Khan Shaykhun in April 2017
- Rebels led by Hayat Tahrir al-Sham entered Damascus on 8 December 2024; Assad fled to Russia
- As of 2025 he lives in Russia and has not faced trial
