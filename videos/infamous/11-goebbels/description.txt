Joseph Goebbels never commanded an army or ran a camp. As Hitler's propaganda minister from 1933 to 1945, he controlled what Germans read, heard and watched, and used it to make hatred of Jews feel normal. This documentary tells how a rejected writer became the voice of the Nazi regime.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 29 October 1897 in Rheydt, in the Rhineland
- Rejected for military service in 1914 because of a deformed foot
- Earned a doctorate from Heidelberg University in 1921
- Became Nazi party chief (Gauleiter) of Berlin in November 1926
- Appointed Reich Minister of Public Enlightenment and Propaganda in March 1933
- His speech in Munich on 9 November 1938 set off the Kristallnacht pogrom
- Gave the 'total war' speech at the Berlin Sportpalast on 18 February 1943
- Named chancellor in Hitler's testament; on 1 May 1945 he and Magda Goebbels had their six children killed and then took their own lives
