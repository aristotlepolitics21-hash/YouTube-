Genghis Khan began life as an abandoned outcast on the Mongolian steppe and died ruling an empire from northern China to the Caspian Sea. His conquests killed on a scale few events in history can match; his empire also connected the world as never before. This documentary tells both stories.

Key facts in this film:
- Born Temüjin around 1162 near the Onon River, in present-day Mongolia
- Proclaimed Genghis Khan at a great assembly on the Onon in 1206
- Invaded the Jin dynasty of northern China in 1211; its capital Zhongdu (Beijing) fell in 1215
- A Mongol trade caravan was massacred at Otrar, in the Khwarazmian Empire, in 1218
- Invaded the Khwarazmian Empire in 1219; Bukhara, Samarkand, Urgench, Merv and Nishapur fell by 1221
- Died in August 1227 during a campaign against the Western Xia; his burial place is unknown
- His successors created the largest contiguous land empire in history
- A 2003 genetic study suggested about 0.5% of men worldwide carry a Y-chromosome lineage possibly descended from his family
