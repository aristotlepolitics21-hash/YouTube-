On 11 September 2001, al-Qaeda hijackers killed 2,977 people in the United States. This documentary tells how Osama bin Laden, the son of a Saudi construction magnate, built a terrorist network, declared war on America, and was finally found and killed in Pakistan in 2011, and what his attacks set in motion.

Key facts in this film:
- Born in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in 1957
- Founded al-Qaeda with a small group of followers in 1988
- Stripped of Saudi citizenship in 1994; moved from Sudan to Afghanistan in 1996
- Signed a February 1998 statement calling on Muslims to kill Americans and their allies, civilians included
- Al-Qaeda bombed the US embassies in Kenya and Tanzania on 7 August 1998, killing 224 people
- The 11 September 2001 attacks killed 2,977 people, not counting the 19 hijackers
- Escaped US and Afghan forces at Tora Bora in December 2001
- Killed by US special forces in Abbottabad, Pakistan, on 2 May 2011 (local time)
