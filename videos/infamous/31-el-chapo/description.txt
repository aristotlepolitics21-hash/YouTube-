Joaquín 'El Chapo' Guzmán grew up poor in the mountains of Sinaloa and built one of the largest drug-trafficking organisations in the world. He escaped from maximum-security prisons twice before he was extradited to the United States, where a jury convicted him in 2019. This documentary tells how he rose, how he was caught, and what his cartel left behind.

Key facts in this film:
- Born, by most accounts, in 1957 in La Tuna, Badiraguato, Sinaloa, Mexico
- Arrested in Guatemala in 1993 and imprisoned in Mexico
- Escaped from Puente Grande prison on 19 January 2001
- Recaptured in Mazatlán on 22 February 2014
- Escaped from Altiplano prison through a tunnel about 1.5 km long on 11 July 2015
- Recaptured in Los Mochis on 8 January 2016; extradited to the US on 19 January 2017
- Convicted on all ten counts in Brooklyn federal court on 12 February 2019
- Sentenced on 17 July 2019 to life plus 30 years and ordered to forfeit $12.6 billion
