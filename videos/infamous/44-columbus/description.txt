Christopher Columbus crossed the Atlantic four times and opened the Americas to European colonisation. For the Taíno people who met him, it was the beginning of a catastrophe. This documentary tells both stories: the navigator, and the governor sent home in chains.

Key facts in this film:
- Born in Genoa in 1451, the son of a wool weaver
- Sailed from Palos, Spain, on 3 August 1492 with three ships and about 90 men
- Made landfall on 12 October 1492 on an island in the Bahamas its people called Guanahani
- Made four voyages to the Americas between 1492 and 1504
- In 1500 royal investigator Francisco de Bobadilla arrested him and sent him to Spain in chains
- Bobadilla's report was rediscovered in the archive at Simancas, Spain, and published in 2006
- Died in Valladolid, Spain, on 20 May 1506
- Columbus Day became a US national holiday in 1937; in 2021 a US president first proclaimed Indigenous Peoples' Day
