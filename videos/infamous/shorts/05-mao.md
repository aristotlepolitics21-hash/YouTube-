# 05 · Mao Zedong: "The harvests were lies"

**Format:** vertical 9:16 · 60 s · 4 scenes × 15 s · Zack D Films–style hyper-real 3D horror: smooth
grey-skinned characters, cold desaturated grade with one blood-red accent, flickering practical light,
deep shadows, slow creeping push-ins broken by violent edits. Word-by-word captions, centre screen, white
with the key word in red. Fast, low, calm narration, about 40 words per scene. Horror through dread,
sound and implication: no gore, and no victims shown being harmed.

**Edit grammar:** `[RAMP]` speed ramp · `[GLITCH]` 4-frame RGB-split glitch cut · `[FLASH]` 2-frame white
flash · `[PUNCH]` 110% zoom punch on the beat · `[FREEZE]` freeze-frame with red circle · `[SMASH]` smash
cut to silence · `[SHAKE]` camera shake · `[J]` J-cut, next scene's sound starts early.

**Loop:** the last line flows into the first ("…But look up in Beijing. / His portrait still hangs over Tiananmen…"),
so the Short replays seamlessly.

## Scene 1 · 0:00–0:15 · The face on the gate

**Visual**
- 0:00–0:02 · Pitch black. Floodlights bang on: extreme close-up of a giant painted eye on a vast portrait. The paint glistens. `[PUNCH]`
- 0:02–0:05 · Pull back: the portrait hangs over an empty Tiananmen Square at night. The painted eyes shift slowly to the camera. `[FREEZE]` red circle on the eyes.
- 0:05–0:10 · A banknote on a bare table. The face printed on it is in red ink, and the ink starts to run down the paper. `[GLITCH]`
- 0:10–0:15 · Dive through the banknote `[RAMP]` into the countryside at dusk: an empty village, rice bowls upturned on every doorstep, a dry field full of small mounds of earth. `[SHAKE]` as the camera lands.

**On-screen text:** THE DEADLIEST FAMINE IN RECORDED HISTORY.

**VO (38 words)**
> His portrait still hangs over Tiananmen Square. His face is on China's banknotes. Millions visit his
> embalmed body. Yet his Great Leap Forward caused the deadliest famine in recorded history. Tens of
> millions of people died. Here's how.

**Sound:** silence → floodlight clank · wet paint creaking · sub-bass drop on the eyes · a cash counter ticking, slowing · wind over the fields, one heartbeat.

## Scene 2 · 0:15–0:30 · The leap

**Visual**
- 0:15–0:18 · A village at night lit orange by hundreds of backyard furnaces. Grey figures carry pots and doors toward the flames in perfect step. `[J]` · `[RAMP]`
- 0:18–0:21 · Close on a cooking pot sagging into slag, then hardening into a useless grey lump. `[PUNCH]`
- 0:21–0:26 · A dim office. An official's sweating face; he writes a harvest figure, crosses it out, writes a bigger one, again and again. The numbers on the page swell and burn red. `[GLITCH]` on each rewrite.
- 0:26–0:30 · Grain sacks thrown onto a truck under a floodlight. Behind it, a granary door swings shut on an empty village. `[SMASH]`

**On-screen text:** THE HARVESTS WERE LIES.

**VO (42 words)**
> In 1958, Mao set out to overtake Britain in steel. Pots, tools and doors were melted in backyard
> furnaces. Afraid of being called rightists, officials reported impossible harvests. The state took
> grain based on the lies. Villages were left with almost nothing.

**Sound:** furnace roar · metal groaning · pen scratch speeding up into a shriek of tinnitus · truck door slam → dead silence.

## Scene 3 · 0:30–0:45 · The warning

**Visual**
- 0:30–0:33 · A mountain conference hall at Lushan. One man rises from a row of seated officials; slowly, every head in the room turns toward him. `[PUNCH]`
- 0:33–0:37 · His chair, now empty, his nameplate slid away. The others stare straight ahead, mouths shut, as the lights above them die one by one. `[GLITCH]`
- 0:37–0:41 · A train of grain wagons rolls past an empty station at night. The camera doesn't follow it; it stays on the empty platform. `[RAMP]`
- 0:41–0:45 · A map of China. Red spreads across the provinces like veins as numbers burn in: 15,000,000… 30,000,000… 45,000,000. `[FLASH]` on each number.

**On-screen text:** ONE MAN WARNED HIM. THEN SILENCE.

**VO (40 words)**
> In 1959, the defence minister privately warned Mao the policy was failing. Mao purged him. Officials
> learned to stay silent. Grain exports continued. Estimates of the dead range from fifteen to over
> forty-five million. Many demographers say around thirty million.

**Sound:** chair scrape, amplified · the room's hum cutting out light by light · train wheels like a slow heartbeat · a sub-bass hit on each number.

- 0:56–1:00 · The camera tilts up from the dark field into the floodlit portrait over Tiananmen Square from 0:00. `[FLASH]` back to the start for the loop.

**On-screen text:** THE FACE HONOURED. THE GRAVES UNMARKED.

**VO (41 words)**
> Then the Cultural Revolution tore the country apart. His defenders point to a unified China, equality
> for women and longer lives. Critics answer no achievement required tens of millions of deaths. Many
> famine graves lie unmarked. But look up in Beijing.

**Sound:** chanting slowed and detuned · a ticking clock under the split frame · total silence over the field · one detuned piano note, then the wet paint creak from 0:00 for the loop.
