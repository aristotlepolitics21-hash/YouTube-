Benito Mussolini founded the fascist movement in 1919 and ruled Italy as dictator from 1922 to 1943. He crushed opposition at home, waged brutal colonial wars in Libya and Ethiopia, passed racial laws against Italy's Jews, and took his country into a disastrous war as Hitler's ally. This documentary tells how he took power, and how it ended.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 29 July 1883 near Predappio, Italy
- Expelled from the Italian Socialist Party in 1914 for supporting entry into the First World War
- Founded the Fasci di Combattimento in Milan on 23 March 1919
- Appointed prime minister by King Victor Emmanuel III after the March on Rome, October 1922
- Socialist deputy Giacomo Matteotti was kidnapped and murdered by fascists on 10 June 1924
- Italy invaded Ethiopia on 3 October 1935 and used poison gas
- Racial laws against Italy's Jews were passed in 1938
- Deposed and arrested on 25 July 1943; shot by partisans on 28 April 1945
