Between 1974 and 1978, Ted Bundy murdered young women and girls across several US states, from Washington to Florida, escaping from custody twice along the way. This documentary centres the people whose lives he took, the survivors who fought back, and the ordinary police work that finally stopped him.

Key facts in this film:
- Born Theodore Robert Cowell on 24 November 1946 in Burlington, Vermont
- Janice Ott and Denise Naslund were abducted from Lake Sammamish State Park, Washington, on 14 July 1974
- Carol DaRonch escaped from him in Murray, Utah, on 8 November 1974
- Arrested by Utah Highway Patrol Sgt Bob Hayward on 16 August 1975; convicted of kidnapping DaRonch in 1976
- Escaped from custody in Aspen (June 1977) and Glenwood Springs (December 1977), Colorado
- Lisa Levy and Margaret Bowman were murdered at the Chi Omega house, Florida State University, on 15 January 1978
- Kimberly Leach, 12, was abducted in Lake City, Florida, on 9 February 1978
- Arrested in Pensacola on 15 February 1978; executed at Florida State Prison on 24 January 1989
