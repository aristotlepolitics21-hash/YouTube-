Pablo Escobar built the Medellín cartel into one of the richest criminal organisations in history, and when Colombia tried to extradite him, he went to war with the state. He bombed a passenger jet, killed a presidential candidate, judges, journalists and hundreds of police officers. This documentary tells how he rose, how he fell, and why Medellín now remembers his victims instead of him.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 1 December 1949 in Rionegro, Antioquia, Colombia
- Arrested with cocaine in 1976; the case collapsed
- Elected an alternate member of Colombia's Congress in 1982; forced out in 1984
- Justice Minister Rodrigo Lara Bonilla was assassinated on 30 April 1984
- Presidential candidate Luis Carlos Galán was assassinated on 18 August 1989
- Avianca Flight 203 was bombed on 27 November 1989, killing 107 on board and 3 on the ground
- Surrendered in June 1991 to La Catedral prison; escaped in July 1992
- Shot dead by Colombian police in Medellín on 2 December 1993, a day after his 44th birthday
