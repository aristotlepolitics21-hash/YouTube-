Reinhard Gehlen ran the German army's intelligence on the Soviet Union for Hitler. Within a year of Germany's defeat he was working for the Americans, and for twenty-two years he ran West Germany's foreign intelligence, hiring former SS and Gestapo men along the way. This documentary tells how the Cold War turned Hitler's spymaster into an ally, and what that bargain cost.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 3 April 1902 in Erfurt, Germany
- Head of Foreign Armies East, the army's intelligence department for the Soviet front, from April 1942
- Dismissed by Hitler in April 1945; surrendered to US forces in Bavaria on 22 May 1945
- Ran the Gehlen Organization under US Army control from 1946; the CIA took it over in 1949
- First president of West Germany's Federal Intelligence Service (BND) from 1 April 1956 to 1968
- BND officer and former SS man Heinz Felfe was arrested as a Soviet spy in November 1961 and sentenced to 14 years in 1963
- CIA files released in 2006 show West German intelligence told the CIA in 1958 that Adolf Eichmann was living in Argentina under a false name
- Died 8 June 1979 in Berg on Lake Starnberg, Bavaria
