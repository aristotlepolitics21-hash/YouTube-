For four decades Enver Hoxha ruled Albania as the most isolated communist state on earth. He banned religion, forbade travel, jailed tens of thousands and covered the country in concrete bunkers against an invasion that never came. This documentary tells how he did it, and what Albanians lived through.

Key facts in this film:
- Born 16 October 1908 in Gjirokastër, then in the Ottoman Empire
- Leader of the Albanian Communist Party from the early 1940s; head of government from 1944
- Broke in turn with Yugoslavia (1948), the Soviet Union (1961) and China (1978)
- Declared Albania the world's first atheist state in 1967
- Around 173,000 concrete bunkers were built across Albania under his rule
- Albanian estimates put political executions at around 6,000, with tens of thousands imprisoned or interned
- Died 11 April 1985 in Tirana, aged 76
- His statue in Tirana was pulled down on 20 February 1991
