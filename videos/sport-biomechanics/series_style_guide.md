# Sport Biomechanics: series format

The rules every new video in the series follows.

## Look
- 3D clay-white mannequin, dark navy night stadium, Pitchwolf-style.
- Title box at top: white line (athlete or event) + orange line (hook).
- Big burned-in captions under the title; numbers and key words in orange.
- Joint dots and bone lines, a centre-of-mass dot, and badges for key numbers.
- 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, about 50–70 s, AI voiceover.
- Facts checked before scripting. Anything only *reported* is said that way on screen.

## Structure
1. **Hook** (0–5 s): record or number + freeze-frame.
2. **Context**: who, when, why it matters.
3. **Biomechanics**: 3–4 beats, one mechanism each (technique, body, timing).
4. **⚛ THE PHYSICS BEHIND IT**: new, required in every video from now on.
5. **Payoff / ending**: back to the record, plus a loop or "Part 2" hook.

## "The Physics Behind It" segment (required)
- About 10–15 s, placed just before the payoff.
- Its own title card: **⚛ THE PHYSICS** with an orange underline.
- One law or equation per video, shown on screen in a clean panel,
  then **applied to the athlete with real numbers** (worked example).
- A visual of the physics in the 3D scene (vectors, energy bars, arcs, forces).
- One line saying what it means in plain words.

Example topics:
| Event | Physics | Worked example |
|---|---|---|
| Pole vault | Energy: ½mv² → mgh | 9.5 m/s run-up → h = v²/2g ≈ 4.6 m centre-of-mass rise |
| Javelin | Projectile + aerodynamic lift/drag | Release speed, angle and angle of attack |
| Shot put | Projectile with release height | Optimum angle below 45° because release is ~2 m up |
| Sprint start | Impulse = force × time | Block push force and time give exit speed |
| Swimming | Drag = ½ρC_dAv² | 2× speed → 4× drag; power ∝ v³ |
| Hammer throw | Centripetal force F = mv²/r | Force on the arms at release speed |
| Gymnastics / diving | Angular momentum L = Iω | Tuck shrinks I so spin rate ω rises |
| Cycling | Power vs air drag P ∝ v³ | Why drafting saves so much |
| Football free kick | Magnus effect | Spin bends the ball's path |
