## Honest cost
- **Totals:** 6 ready, 3 cheap re-cuts, 21 new 3D builds.
- **Your time:** about 30–45 min per video to watch, check flagged facts, add a trending sound, and post with the caption. That's about 15–20 h over the month.
- **Build time:** about 1–2 h per new video, with a render of 10–25 min. The **first video in a new setting** (gym, rink, ski hill, velodrome, ring, boat, pitch) takes 2–3 h.
- **Plan:** use week 1's buffer to build days 7–13 ahead. Stay at least 3 days ahead after that.

**If the month goes wrong, drop in this order:**
1. **Cheap re-cuts:** days 14, 21 and 28 become optional.
2. **Videos that need a new setting:** move days 20, 25 and 27 (ski hill, velodrome, rowing) to month 2.
3. **Posting pace:** go to every other day. You still keep the series running.
