Scripts, edits and scenes live in `render3d/episodes/<module>.json` and `render3d/<module>.js`.
Storyboards (one frame per clip) are in `render3d/storyboards/<module>.jpg`.

Render one:

```sh
python3 render3d/make_episode.py render3d/episodes/sneeze.json --voice voices/en_US-ryan-high.onnx -o render3d/out/sneeze.mp4 --workers 3
```

Suggested upload order: the strongest hooks first, then alternate gross and safe topics. Each short ends on a fact or a safety line rather than a "follow" ask. Put the series CTA in the pinned comment.

## 8. Eye floaters (`floaters`)

**Description**

```
Floaters are clumps of collagen fibers in the clear jelly (vitreous) inside your eye. They cast shadows on your retina, and they drift because the jelly sloshes when your eye moves. Usually harmless, but a sudden shower of new floaters or flashes of light can mean a torn retina. See an eye doctor the same day.

3D animation. Narration and captions are synthetic. Not medical advice.

## 9. Sunburn (`sunburn`)

**Description**

```
UV light dives into your skin and kinks the DNA in your skin cells. Cells too damaged to fix destroy themselves, and widened blood vessels make the skin red, hot and painful. Days later the dead layer peels. Some damaged cells survive, and that damage adds up and raises your skin-cancer risk. Sunscreen blocks much of it.

3D animation. Narration and captions are synthetic. Not medical advice.

## 10. Ears pop on a plane (`earspop`)

**Description**

```
Behind your eardrum is a small pocket of air. As a plane climbs, cabin pressure drops, the trapped air pushes the eardrum outward, and sounds get muffled. Swallowing or yawning pops open the Eustachian tube, air rushes through, the pressure evens out: pop. A cold can clog that tube, which is why flying with one can hurt.

3D animation. Narration and captions are synthetic. Not medical advice.

#shorts #science #travel
```
